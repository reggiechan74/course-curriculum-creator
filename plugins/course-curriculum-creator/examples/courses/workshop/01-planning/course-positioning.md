# Course Positioning

## Course: Lease Basics

## Positioning Statement

A one-day, in-person workshop that teaches new property administrators to read a commercial lease, find the clauses that drive cash flow, and abstract them accurately.

## Target Audience

Junior property administrators and leasing coordinators with less than one year of commercial real estate experience.

## Outcomes

Participants leave able to name the parts of a lease, explain how base rent and operating cost recoveries work, and abstract a short sample lease.
