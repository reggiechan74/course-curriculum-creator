# Course Positioning

## Course: Cap Rates for New Analysts

## Positioning Statement

A short self-paced course that explains capitalization rates well enough for a new analyst to calculate one, read one and question one.

## Target Audience

New analysts and associates who see cap rates in every deal memo but have never derived one.

## Outcomes

Learners can calculate a cap rate from net operating income and price, interpret market cap rates, and spot a weak cap rate assumption.
