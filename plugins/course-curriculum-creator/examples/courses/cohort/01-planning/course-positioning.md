# Course Positioning

## Course: Underwriting Lab

## Positioning Statement

A four-session virtual cohort in which analysts build, stress and defend an acquisition underwriting model for a single multifamily asset.

## Target Audience

Real estate analysts with one to three years of experience who build models but rarely defend them.

## Outcomes

Participants leave with a working underwriting model and the ability to explain its assumptions to an investment committee.
